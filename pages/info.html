About

As an independent designer, I have collaborated with publishing houses, architecture and design studios, and independent artists.

Experience

I worked as Art Director at Bisiesto, the daily journal of the ICAIC Young Filmmakers Festival, from 2015 to 2018. From 2017 to 2021, I was also Art Director at Cine Cubano Magazine, published by the Cuban Institute of Cinematographic Art and Industry (ICAIC).

Education

Bachelor’s Degree in Visual Communication Design from the Higher Institute of Design of Havana, Cuba (2009–2014), followed by a Postgraduate Diploma in Color Management in Design from the same institution (2014).
